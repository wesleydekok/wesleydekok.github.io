About IRIS

More than a study association

IRIS is the study association of Zuyd University of Applied Sciences, founded to support students and teachers, strengthen the community and provide a place where everyone is welcome.

Whether you are looking for study support, fun activities, or simply a place to meet like-minded people — you are welcome at IRIS.
